# Interview notes

Be able to explain:

- Why PowerShell: native Windows administration and security telemetry.
- Why 10 modules: separation of assessment domains.
- Why 50 functions: deterministic, testable checks with one clear purpose each.
- Why read-only: safe and reproducible endpoint assessment.
- Why ISO wording: evidence is mapped to security themes; the project does not claim organisational compliance.
- Why Al Shad Real Estate: fictional small-business case used for realistic business context.
